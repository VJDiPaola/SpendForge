# SpendForge backup video plan

**Status:** Plan only. Recording and publication require separate authorization.
**Target:** A 60–90 second local backup matching the judged demo script without rewriting provider chronology.

## Recording gate

Do not record the integration-complete version until all of the following are true:

- a protected Preview loads at the fixed demo viewport;
- the truth labels match the current ledger;
- any claimed Rain purchase has authoritative direct readback;
- any claimed Monad purchase has a facilitator or chain transaction reference;
- the artifact and ledger work after refresh;
- the fixture and any verified replay are visibly distinct;
- no secret, full provider identifier, wallet private key, PAN, CVC, notification, bookmark, or personal tab can enter frame.

If those gates are not met, a product-progress recording may use the current script, but its title and captions must say `Live bounded model proof + fixture walkthrough + partial Rain Sandbox evidence`; it cannot be presented as a completed two-rail integration.

## Capture setup

- Capture one browser window at 1920×1080 or 1440×900 with zoom fixed at 100%.
- Warm `/`, the Atlas mission, `/ledger`, and the Atlas artifact before recording.
- Keep notifications off and close unrelated applications.
- Record a clean microphone track; product remains the dominant visual.
- Keep the cursor still during narration and move only for the next named action.
- Leave one second of clean handle before the first word and after the last screen.

## Required shots

1. Landing proof posture.
2. Live bounded model receipt and deterministic policy verification.
3. Mission budget plus decline/block in the five-step fixture timeline.
4. Rain settlement safe-stop: card/readback and authorization matched; one settlement POST returned HTTP 400 and bounded readbacks remained nonterminal.
5. Monad proof card or clearly labeled unproven state.
6. Expanded ledger receipt and audit JSON availability.
7. Final artifact truth bar and evidence strip.

## Edit rules

- Cut dead time; never reorder events into a false provider sequence.
- Do not replace a provider wait with a success screen from a different attempt.
- Captions must preserve `Rain Sandbox`, `Monad Testnet`, `Fixture`, and `Synthetic` labels.
- Use restrained zooms only around the policy decision, provider state, and proof link.
- No sponsor logo montage, fake terminal output, decorative wallet balance, or simulated explorer transaction.

## Recovery ladder

1. **Provider latency:** show the real waiting or reconciliation state and continue to product evidence.
2. **Provider outage:** show the labeled unavailable-provider view.
3. **Verified replay exists:** use it only with `Verified run replay` visible throughout.
4. **No verified replay:** use the fixture walkthrough and say so; do not promote it to provider proof.
5. **Preview unavailable:** use the local production build plus the architecture/judging map, labeled as local build evidence.

## Output and verification

- Proposed local file: `output/demo/spendforge-backup.mp4`.
- Export a common H.264 MP4 and keep the original capture.
- Watch the full export with sound on, then muted with captions.
- Verify all small labels at normal playback size.
- Verify the eventual share link while logged out before submission.
- Keep the local file until the judging window closes.
